Weight Tracker (Weightly)

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Weight Tracker app for Android (also called Weightly, "the app") handles your information. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the app's developer.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your phone. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • We collect nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet. It never sends your data to us or to anyone else.
  • You're in control. Data only leaves your device if you back it up, export it or share it yourself.

1. Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device:

  • Weight entries: each weight you log, the date and time it was logged, and any note you add.
  • Settings: your weight unit (kg or lbs), theme, chart preferences and reminder schedule (time and days).

This information is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. We never receive it.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal or health information. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

3. Permissions the app uses

PermissionWhy
Notifications Shows your weigh-in reminders. The app only asks for this when you turn reminders on, and you can turn it off at any time in Android settings.
Run at startup Restores your reminder schedule after your phone restarts or the app is updated.
Vibration Lets a reminder notification vibrate, if your phone's settings allow it.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other files. The only exception is a file you choose yourself when restoring a backup.

4. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by Android on your device. No information is sent anywhere to deliver them.

5. Backup, restore and export

  • Backup creates a file (.json) with your entries and settings. You choose where to save it using Android's file picker.
  • Restore reads a backup file that you select and adds its entries to the app. It doesn't change your current settings.
  • Export to CSV creates a spreadsheet file of your entries and opens Android's share menu. You choose which app, if any, receives it.

Once you save or share a file, it is handled by the place or app you chose (for example, a cloud drive or email app) under that service's privacy policy. We never receive these files.

6. Android device backup

If you have turned on Android's built-in backup (for example, "Backup by Google One"), Android may include this app's data in your device backup, stored in your own Google account. This is managed by Android and Google, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

7. Links that open outside the app

The app has links to this policy and a "Report Bug" option. Tapping them opens your web browser or email app. If you send us an email, we receive only what you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply to you and fix the problem, and we delete it when it's no longer needed.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • To delete one entry, swipe it away in the Records list.
  • To delete everything, use Delete All Records in the app's menu, or uninstall the app.
  • Backup or export files you've saved elsewhere are not deleted by the app. Delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

9. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. If someone else can unlock your phone, they can open the app and see your entries. Keep your device locked and your backup files in a safe place.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Health information

Weight is personal health information. That's why the app keeps it only on your device and never transmits it. The app is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example, by adding a new feature that uses the internet, we will update this policy and its effective date before that change is released.
