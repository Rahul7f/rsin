Privacy Policy — Blood Sugar Log

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Blood Sugar Log is a simple diary for logging and tracking blood sugar readings that you type in yourself. In short: your readings stay on your phone. The only part of the app that uses the internet is the advertising.

1. Your health data stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. That includes readings, dates and times, "when" labels (e.g. Fasting), tags, notes, your unit, your target range and your reminders.

  • The app has no account, no sign-in, no server and no cloud sync.
  • We (the developer) cannot see your data. We never receive it and have no copy of it.
  • We do not sell, share or use your health data for advertising.
  • Reminders are local notifications that your phone schedules itself. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Doctor report (PDF)

When you create a PDF report, it is made on your phone. It only leaves your phone if you choose to share or save it (e.g. by email or a messaging app). After that, the app or service you picked handles it under its own privacy policy.

Backups

Your phone's own system backup (e.g. Google or iCloud device backup) may include the app's data, depending on your phone settings. That backup is handled by Google or Apple, not by us.

2. No analytics or tracking by us

The app has no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. This is the only network traffic the app makes. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device identifiers, including your phone's advertising ID
  • general device and app information (e.g. model, operating system, app version, language)
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • information about how you interact with ads

AdMob does not receive your readings, notes or any other health data from the app.

Consent (UK, EEA and Switzerland): before any ads load, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can choose personalised or non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Other places: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. How to delete your data

  • In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete all data. This permanently removes every reading, reminder and setting from your phone.
  • Uninstall the app: this also deletes all app data from your phone.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

5. Not a medical device

The app is for logging only and is not a medical device. It does not measure blood sugar, diagnose or treat anything, or give medical advice. Talk to your doctor about your readings.

6. Children

The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
