Progressive Overload

WeekAction
1Learn proper form
2+2.5 kg per exercise if all sets done with good form
3+2.5 kg again where possible
4Same weight, perfect technique (deload if fatigued)
5+Repeat the cycle

12 reps comfortable on every set → increase weight (smallest increment). Can't complete 8 reps → reduce weight.

Carbs & Fats

  • Fruits
  • Almonds, Walnuts, Peanuts
  • Flax / Chia seeds

Foods to Limit

  • Cold drinks
  • Packaged fruit juice
  • Chips & namkeen
  • Sweets & desserts (occasional treat only)
  • Deep-fried snacks (samosa, kachori, pakora)
  • Fast food (pizza, burgers, fries)

Weekly Cheat Meal

One cheat meal per week — not a full cheat day.

Examples: paneer tikka with naan, dosa, biryani, or pizza in moderation. Eat until satisfied, not overly full — then back to normal diet at the next meal.
