Child Safety & Anti-CSAE Policy

Naughty Time  |  Effective Date: May 9, 2025  |  Last Updated: May 9, 2025

Zero Tolerance. Naughty Time has an absolute zero-tolerance policy toward any content, behavior, or activity that sexually exploits or abuses children in any form. This policy is non-negotiable and enforced without exception.

📋 About This Policy

This Child Safety & Anti-CSAE (Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation) Policy applies to Naughty Time, an 18+ adult party game application published on Google Play Store.

This policy exists to clearly communicate our absolute commitment to child safety, to comply with Google Play's Child Safety Standards (mandatory since March 2025), and to meet all applicable laws and regulations regarding the protection of minors.

This document is publicly accessible and forms part of our official Terms of Service and community standards.

🚫 Strict Prohibition of CSAE Content

Naughty Time explicitly and unconditionally prohibits all of the following:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind — images, video, text, or audio
  • Content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors in any form
  • Grooming behavior — any attempt by an adult to gain the trust of a minor for exploitative purposes
  • Solicitation of minors for sexual purposes
  • Sharing, distributing, or linking to CSAM or CSAE-related content
  • Content that normalizes, glorifies, or encourages the sexual abuse of children
  • Any interaction between adults and minors of a sexual or exploitative nature
⚠️ Any user found engaging in or distributing any of the above will be permanently banned immediately and reported to law enforcement and the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority.

🔞 This App Is Strictly 18+ Only

Naughty Time is designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and above. We have implemented multiple layers of protection to prevent minors from accessing the app:

Protections We Have

  • 18+ age confirmation gate on first launch
  • Adults Only content rating via IARC questionnaire
  • "Restrict Declared Minors" enabled in Play Console
  • No content targeting or appealing to children
  • No child-friendly imagery, characters, or themes

What We Do Not Allow

  • Minors creating accounts or using the app
  • Content designed to appeal to users under 18
  • Any marketing or targeting directed at minors
  • Bypassing or circumventing the age gate
  • Misrepresenting age to gain access
✓ Parents are strongly advised to use Android's parental control features and Google Family Link to prevent minors from accessing this application.

⚖️ How We Enforce This Policy

When a violation of this policy is identified — whether through user reports, internal review, or notification from Google Play — we take the following steps immediately:

1
Immediate Account Suspension

The reported user's account is suspended instantly pending investigation. They lose all access to the app.

2
Content Removal

Any violating content is removed from our systems within 24 hours of discovery or report.

3
Investigation

We review all available information related to the violation. No exception is made regardless of account history or subscription status.

4
Reporting to Authorities

Confirmed CSAM or CSAE is reported immediately to NCMEC (National Center for Missing and Exploited Children) via CyberTipline, or the relevant regional authority in the user's country, including NCRB in India.

5
Permanent Ban

The offending account is permanently terminated. Device identifiers are flagged to prevent re-registration.

6
Google Play Notification

We notify Google Play of the violation and the action taken, as required under the Child Safety Standards policy.

  • Report button accessible from within the app at all times
  • Reports are reviewed within 24 hours

  • Users are not penalized for making good-faith reports
