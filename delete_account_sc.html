Request Account Deletion

Last Updated: July 2025

You have the right to request deletion of your Stranger Chat account and associated data. Please read the information below before submitting your request.

This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered. All your points, friends, and profile data will be lost.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support team using the address below.
  2. Use the subject line exactly as shown.
  3. Include your registered email address or Firebase User ID in the message body (if known).
  4. We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation.
Send your request to:
rsinlabs@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request - Stranger Chat

What Gets Deleted

Data Action Notes
Profile (name, photo, gender, preferences) Deleted Removed within 7 days
Points & rating Deleted Removed with account
Friends list Deleted Removed with account
FCM token & notification settings Deleted Removed with account
Firebase Auth account Deleted Sign-in access removed
Chat messages Retained Retained for safety enforcement and legal compliance. May be disclosed to law enforcement if required.
Moderation & ban records Retained Retained permanently for safety enforcement.
User reports filed against you Retained Retained for investigation purposes.
Note: Chat messages and moderation records are retained even after account deletion to comply with legal obligations and support law enforcement investigations where required. This is disclosed in our Privacy Policy.

Premium Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. Please cancel your subscription separately through the Google Play Store before requesting account deletion to avoid further charges.

Questions

For any questions about your data or this process, contact us at rsinlabs@gmail.com.
