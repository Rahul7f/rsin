Privacy Policy

Welcome to TaskBell. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document explains how TaskBell collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

TaskBell is designed with a strict privacy-first approach:

  • Local Data Storage: All your task titles, descriptions, due dates, reminder preferences, and completed task states are stored strictly locally on your device using SQLite/Room local database.
  • No Cloud Servers / No Accounts: TaskBell does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profiles. We do not transmit or store your personal task data on remote servers.
  • No Personal Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information to third parties or advertising networks.

2. Device Permissions Requested and Purpose

To provide reliable, timely task reminders and call-style alerts, TaskBell requests the following Android device permissions:

Notification Access (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used to display timely task notifications and reminders in your device's notification bar.

Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Required exclusively for our Call-Style Alert feature, displaying a full-screen incoming alert UI over the lock screen and active apps when an urgent task alarm triggers.

Exact Alarm Scheduling (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM)

Required to ensure task alarms and timers fire precisely at the exact minute set by the user.

Ignore Battery Optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS)

Optional permission used to prevent OEM background battery management from delaying or killing task alarm execution.

3. Third-Party Services

TaskBell does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral advertising SDKs that collect personal identifiers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. All data remains local to the user's device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon publishing.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@taskbell.app
