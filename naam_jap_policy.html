This Privacy Policy explains how the Naam Jap Counter app ("the app", "we") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your mantras and custom mantras
  • Chant counts, daily totals, streaks and daily goal
  • Reminder times, sound and mala-style preferences, language and other settings

We do not have a server, we do not require an account or login, and we do not collect, upload or sell this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently. The home-screen widget reads the same on-device data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads) provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may automatically collect:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising IDAd serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention, personalised ads (where you allow it)
Approximate location derived from IP addressShowing relevant ads, legal compliance
App interactions with ads (views, clicks), diagnostics and crash data from the ads SDKAd measurement and analytics

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): on first launch the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Notifications: reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your phone; no data leaves the device. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image/text of your progress and hands it to the Android share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience devotional app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested in a way that complies with Google Play Families and AdMob policies.

7. Security and retention

Your jap data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox, until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.
