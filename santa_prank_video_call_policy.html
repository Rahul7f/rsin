Privacy Policy

Santa Video Call · Effective 20 November 2026

Santa Video Call ("the app") is an entertainment app for parents: it shows a pretend video call and chat from Santa Claus using pre-recorded videos and pre-written messages. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Who the app is for

The app is intended for adults (18+) who use it with their children. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent sets up the call and hands the phone to the child.

Information that stays on your device

  • Child's first name you type in, so Santa can greet them. Stored only on the device; never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Camera: used only to show a live preview in the small "you" window during the pretend call. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. The camera is optional.
  • Chat messages typed to Santa are answered by pre-written replies on the device. They are not stored after the chat is closed and never leave the device.
  • Scheduled calls and settings (ringtone, vibration) are stored on the device. Scheduled calls use a local notification; no server is involved.

We do not run any servers and we do not receive any of this information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (a banner on the home screen and an ad after a call ends — never during a call). Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences.

Where required by law (for example in the UK and EU) the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn how Google uses information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

  • Camera – self-view during the pretend call (optional).
  • Notifications – to ring when a scheduled Santa call is due.
  • Vibration, wake lock – to vibrate while ringing and keep the screen on during a call.
  • Internet – only to load ads.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information. Apart from what Google AdMob collects as described above, the app sends no data off the device.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
