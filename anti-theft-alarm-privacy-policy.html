This Privacy Policy explains how the Anti Theft Alarm app ("the app", "we", "us"), published by Rsinlabs, handles information when you use it on your Android device. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or account is required.
  • We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. The app has no server of its own.
  • All app settings, including your PIN, are stored only on your device.
  • The microphone is used only for clap detection, processed live on your device. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data as described in section 4.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your phone so it works the way you set it up. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • Your 4-digit PIN used to stop the alarm
  • Selected protection mode, sensitivity, flash and vibration preferences
  • Chosen alarm sound for each mode and which sounds you have unlocked
  • Language preference

3. Permissions and device features

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language)
  • Ad interactions (ads viewed or clicked) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads

This data is collected directly by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user data

Your choices: Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it is required are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used, and can change that choice later from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. Anyone can reset or delete their Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, from the Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on the device).

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information. Apart from the advertising data collected by Google as described above, the app does not send your information to any third party.

6. Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback or a feature request from the app, your email app opens with a pre-filled message that includes the app version, Android version and device model to help us understand the issue. You can review or remove this before sending. We use your email only to reply to you and improve the app, and we do not share it.

7. Data security and retention

Data saved by the app stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. We do not keep any copy of it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it permanently. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your on-device data is fully under your control. For data collected by Google for advertising, use the controls described in section 4 or Google's privacy tools. For any request, contact us at the email below.
