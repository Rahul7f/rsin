Privacy Policy

Developer: Rsin Labs  |  App: Stranger Chat  |  Last Updated: July 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Rsin Labs ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Stranger Chat mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Age Restriction: Stranger Chat is strictly for users aged 18 years and older. If you are under 18, you must not use this App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

Depending on how you sign up, we collect:

  • Google Sign-In: Display name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google.
  • Anonymous Sign-In: No personal information is collected. A random Firebase Auth UID is assigned.

1.2 Profile Data You Provide

When you set up your profile, we store the following in Firebase Firestore:

  • Display name and username
  • Gender and interest (matching preference)
  • Profile photo URL
  • Date of birth (used for age verification only)
  • Points balance and rating
  • Friend list and friend count
  • Blocked users list
  • FCM token (for push notifications)
  • Account creation date, last login, and last online timestamp
  • Notification preferences

1.3 Chat Messages

Important: Chat messages sent through Stranger Chat are stored permanently in Firebase Firestore. We retain messages to support safety enforcement, investigate reports of abuse or illegal activity, and comply with lawful requests from government authorities or law enforcement in the event of a crime. Messages are not sold, shared commercially, or used for advertising purposes.

1.4 Reports & Moderation Logs

User reports, ban records, and moderation logs (including blocked message attempts) are stored permanently to enforce our community guidelines and support safety investigations.

1.5 Automatically Collected Data

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Approximate location (country level only)
  • App usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Enable anonymous and authenticated chatting and real-time matching
  • Verify that users meet the 18+ age requirement
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent harmful, abusive, or illegal behavior
  • Enforce bans and moderation actions
  • Deliver push notifications
  • Manage points, rewards, and premium subscriptions
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful government or law enforcement requests

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, database, cloud messaging, analytics, and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect anonymous identifiers for ad delivery and frequency capping.
  • Google Play Billing: For managing premium subscriptions. We do not store payment details.
  • Law Enforcement: We will disclose data when required by law, court order, or to comply with a lawful government request — including in cases of suspected criminal activity.
  • NCMEC: Confirmed Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as required by law.

4. Data Retention

  • Chat messages: Stored permanently for safety and legal compliance purposes.
  • User profiles: Retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion requests.
  • Reports & moderation logs: Retained permanently for enforcement purposes.
  • Analytics data: Retained per Firebase's default policies.

5. Data Security

We apply industry-standard security practices including Firebase security rules and encrypted data transmission. However, no online platform can guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to report any suspicious behavior immediately.

6. Your Rights

You may contact us to request:

  • Access to data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate profile data
  • Account deletion (subject to retention obligations for safety and legal compliance)
  • Review of a ban or report

Note: We may be unable to delete data that is required for active safety investigations or legal compliance.

7. Children's Privacy

Stranger Chat is strictly for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that a user is under 18, their account will be immediately terminated and their data removed. See our Child Safety Policy tab for full details.

8. Third-Party Links

The App may allow users to share social media handles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party platforms.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Child Safety & Anti-CSAE Policy

Developer: Rsin Labs  |  App: Stranger Chat  |  Last Updated: July 2025

This Child Safety & Anti-CSAE (Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation) Policy applies to the Stranger Chat mobile application published on Google Play by Rsin Labs. The App is intended only for adults aged 18 and above.

Zero Tolerance: Stranger Chat enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy for any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation. Violations result in immediate permanent bans and reporting to law enforcement.

1. Age Restriction

Stranger Chat is strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow minors to access the App.

We enforce this through:

  • Age verification at signup requiring date of birth confirmation
  • An explicit 18+ acknowledgment checkbox before account activation
  • Immediate termination of any account found to belong to a minor

We explicitly prohibit:

  • Access to the App by users under 18
  • Any depictions of minors in sexual, exploitative, or harmful contexts
  • Grooming, solicitation, or predatory behavior toward minors
  • Sharing or requesting images or content involving minors

2. Zero-Tolerance for CSAE & CSAM

Stranger Chat enforces a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any content involving minors in sexual, exploitative, or abusive contexts is strictly and permanently prohibited.

If CSAE or CSAM is detected, Stranger Chat will:

  • Immediately remove the content
  • Permanently ban the violating user with no right of appeal
  • Preserve all relevant data for law enforcement
  • Report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the appropriate local authority as required by law
  • Fully cooperate with any resulting law enforcement investigation

3. Moderation & Prevention

To protect users and prevent CSAE, Stranger Chat implements the following safety measures:

  • Age verification gate at signup — date of birth required, minimum age enforced
  • Client-side message filtering — messages containing prohibited content are blocked before sending
  • Moderation logs — blocked message attempts are logged with user ID and timestamp for investigation
  • In-app reporting — users can report any inappropriate content or behavior with one tap
  • Manual review of all user reports by the Rsin Labs team
  • Permanent bans for confirmed violations
  • Community guidelines displayed at signup prohibiting sexual and harmful content

4. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report inappropriate content or suspected CSAE within the App using the built-in Report User feature available during any chat session.

You may also contact our child safety representative directly:

Email: rsinlabs@gmail.com

All child safety reports receive the highest response priority and are acted upon immediately.

5. Cooperation With Authorities

Rsin Labs complies with all applicable local, regional, and national laws regarding child safety. If CSAE or CSAM is confirmed, we will:

  • Preserve all required data for law enforcement
  • Report to NCMEC or the relevant legal authority in the applicable jurisdiction
  • Fully support lawful investigations and respond to legal requests promptly

6. Data Retention for Safety

Chat messages and moderation logs are stored permanently to support safety enforcement and to comply with lawful requests from government or law enforcement authorities. This data is not used for commercial purposes.

7. Commitment to a Safe Community

Rsin Labs is committed to maintaining a safe platform for all eligible adult users. Child safety is our highest enforcement priority. Violations are acted upon immediately and without exception.
